# 02 Symptom-based service menu (P1)

**Reference:** Bumper Buddies. **Status:** REMOVED from the site (too much scrolling). Existing services cards stay; this adds a second entry point.

## Requirements
- New "What's wrong with your car?" block grouping common symptoms, each linking to the matching service card and a prefilled WhatsApp message: squeaking brakes, check-engine light, AC not cooling, car pulling to one side, hard starting/weak battery, dents and scratches, vibration at speed, strange engine noise.
- Group headings: Routine maintenance, Diagnostics, Body and paint.
- Optional later: before/after photos for body work in the gallery (needs real photos from the owner).

## Acceptance
- Each symptom maps to at least one existing service id.
- Works in English and Marathi.
